Frequently Asked Questions

How does the heart system work?

You get 10 free hearts every day. Each correct puzzle answer costs 1 heart. Wrong answers don't cost hearts — they go to the Graveyard for free unlimited review. The Daily Challenge gives you 3 free puzzles per day with no heart cost.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the Premium page and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription will be recovered from Apple's servers.

What's the difference between Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime?

Monthly ($4.99/month) and Annual ($29.99/year) give you unlimited hearts and all difficulty levels. Annual saves you 50% compared to monthly. Lifetime ($49.99) is a one-time purchase — everything forever.

My hearts aren't decreasing. Is this a bug?

Check if you're a Pro subscriber (unlimited hearts) or using Daily Challenge free plays (3 per day, no heart cost).
