TileSlash Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect almost nothing. TileSlash is designed to work offline with all your learning data stored locally on your device.

  • Heart count & SRS data: Stored locally using Hive. Never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.
  • Analytics: Anonymous aggregated stats (daily active users, purchase count) are sent to our server for service improvement. No personal identifiers.
  • IAP purchases: Handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information.

2. Data We Don't Collect

  • Email address or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts or social graph
  • Device identifiers
  • Browsing history

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store: Processes in-app purchases. Subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat (optional): Subscription management. Only active if you subscribe to Pro.

4. Your Rights

Since we store data locally, you control it completely. Delete the app to remove all data. Contact us at dcgwdgx@126.com for any privacy questions.
